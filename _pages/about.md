I am a PhD Candidate working with [Nathan Senner](http://www.sennerlab.com/) in the Department of Environmental Conservation at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. I grew up by the Yellow Sea, where I watched shorebirds use the region's shrinking wetlands and found my passion for birds and conservation. From 2018 to 2022, I completed my undergraduate degree in Wildlife, Fish, and Conservation Biology at the University of California, Davis.

Contact: feipenghuang@umass.edu
