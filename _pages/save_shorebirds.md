### Shorebirds are amazing but in steep decline. How can we help?
This list is still being developed and is U.S. biased.

### *Walk around roosting shorebirds and keep your dog on a leash*
*Shorebirds need to rest and refuel during migration. When flushed, they waste energy they need for the next leg of their journey.*

### *Report banded shorebirds!*
*Researchers mark shorebirds with leg flags/bands for individual identification. Keep an eye out for banded shorebirds and help us learn where they go, how long they live, and much more!*

All banded birds can be reported to the [USGS Bird Banding Laboratory](https://www.usgs.gov/labs/bird-banding-laboratory/science/report-a-band).

Report banded American Oystercatchers directly to the [American Oystercatcher Working Group](https://amoywg.org/resighting-and-reporting-bands/)

### *Participate in shorebird community science projects*
[International Shorebird Survey](https://www.manomet.org/sign-up-iss/)

Massachusetts Shorebird Blitz

### *See shorebirds up close at AZA-accredited zoos and aquariums that care for rescued shorebirds*
[Monterey Bay Aquarium](https://www.montereybayaquarium.org/visit/exhibits/sandy-shore-aviary) - wonderful aviary with an ocean backdrop, showcasing the entire coastal system - from shallow seas and sandy dunes to marshes

[National Zoo](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/exhibits/bird-house?tab=1) - Delaware Bay-themed aviary in the historic bird house
